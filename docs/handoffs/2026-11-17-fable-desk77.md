## Changed

- `Session.Parts.cs` (new): `LeftOut`, `PartsLeftOut` (the warning sentence), `StillToFind`, `IfAdded`.
- `LinesBrought` (Session.Choices.cs): what a vendor brings of a request's parts: all of them, or, when it brings every core part, the ones it offers. `VendorReason` refuses only for a missing core part; `PairReason` and the vehicle reasons check a BPA's or IDIQ's scope against what the vendor brings.
- `CheckAward`: a buy from a request with no parts of its own (`Lines` null, as the game sends) takes `LinesBrought`; the arrangement gets those parts, so a part left out still carries its risk (operators and maintenance fail plants more often, DESK-59). A buy that lists its parts is checked as written, which is how the policies and the golden logs play, so neither moved.
- The preview's first warning names the parts left out and who said they were needed (whoever told the shop; else "the request asks for them").
- `RequestStanding.StillToFind` and the sentence's last line, for a request that is arranged, running, short or being bought.
- `CardCoveredNow` (the morning lead) counts any buy in work for the request's site and capability; `Decisions(cardId)` attributes an own-initiative award, a coordination, a J4 ask, a host nation or ACSA request to the request for its site and capability.
- `OrderBy` (DESK-73) names only a route whose vendor brings all of the request.
