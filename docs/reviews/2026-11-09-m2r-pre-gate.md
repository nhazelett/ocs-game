# M2R pre-gate review (Fable, 9 Nov 2026, scheduled run)

The design doc (section 9) says no milestone with a player-facing surface goes to Nick until Fable has seen the opening and each panel as a player would and written down the first two minutes. This is that pass, done before M2R-12 and M2R-17 land. It uses the committed screenshots in `docs/handoffs/images/` (all from 8 Nov, so before M2R-06, M2R-07 and M2R-08 merged) and the Sim's own text on today's main, printed for Spoke B, Spoke B 90, the tutorial and sustainment (turnover) and replayed morning by morning for Spoke B approach 1 and the tutorial's taught path (leads).

## The first two minutes, Spoke B, as a first-time player

1. **Title.** "OCS / Lines of Support", one line under it: take over the contracting shop and keep the deployment supported. New game, Continue greyed, a small Developer tools switch top right. Nothing to misread. (`m2r-10-title.png`)
2. **Before you leave.** Maj Erin Cole, SCO staff, says I have fourteen days before wheels up and can't do everything. Eight choices in her voice with a day cost each, three already ticked, a running count of days used. I know I'm deploying and that this is prep. I don't yet know where. The unticked boxes are dark on dark and hard to see. (`m2r-11-prep.png`)
3. **The turnover.** Capt Sam Whitfield, with a portrait: "Welcome to the island. I fly out in three days." Then who I am (contracting officer for the hub, six hundred people, plus the outer-island sites), where (Spoke B on Second Island, barge twice a week; Spoke C on the host nation grid) and what is due (Spoke B opens day 7, Spoke C day 5). Then the shop, contracts, vendors and money, one paragraph each, ending "Spoke B opens in a week and nobody's bought them power yet. CE's outside." At this point, about ninety seconds in, I can say who I am, where I am and what is due. That is the M2R test, met on paper. (`m2r-11-shop/contracts/vendors/money.png`)
4. **Day 1.** The board leads, large: "Capt Ana Ruiz, 3 CES, is here about power for Spoke B, wanted from day 7." with Open under it; the map shows three islands with their capabilities drawn as things. I know what to do next: open it. (`m2r-13-day1.png`, `m2r-15-day1.png`)
5. **Market.** Open leads to "What are you arranging? Who could provide it? How would you arrange it?", then the quote in sentences, with the fuel gap in amber: "No generator fuel supply line." (`m2r-16.png`)

Verdict: the opening now answers who, where and what is due inside two minutes, and the first decision is obvious. What still stands between this and Nick's gate is below.

## What a first-time player still trips on

### Fable (Sim text), fixed in M2R-19 on this branch
- **The vendor paragraph was a wall.** Nine vendors, each followed by its own confidence phrase, so "I've talked to them, but their prices are estimates" appeared five times in one paragraph (`m2r-11-vendors.png`). Now: where they are by island first, then one sentence per level of what the shop knows, then the outgoing CCO's remarks, then who isn't vetted. Every phrase once.
- **The tutorial's shop paragraph said the same thing twice in two voices.** The authored line ("Two of us, sixteen hours a day between us, ... everything is on paper") ran into the generated one ("There are two of you, 16 hours a day between you. The contract-writing system is down until about day 25"), in a 20-day scenario. The authored line is now just "Here's the shop.", and a system that comes up after the last day reads "won't be up for weeks". The tutorial's money remark, which restated the line above it, is gone.

### Fable (Sim), next: M2R-20
- **Failure leads have no person.** Approach 1's first consequence leads day 11: "Fuel at Spoke B down to 0%: fuel on hand ran out; no arrangement supplies fuel." with no one attached, where every other lead has a face. The site's own customer (Capt Ruiz asked for Spoke B's power) is who would come to the shop about it. M2R-20 gives site-capability board items and leads the person who asked for that capability at that site, else the first customer or commander at the site.

### Codex (game), for M2R-12, M2R-17 or a follow-up
- **The top strip is developer language.** "Day 1 (next)", "Shop: 0 h queued / 16 h/day" and an unlabeled grey bar under Cash. A player reads "(next)" as a button. Suggest "Morning of day 1", "Shop: 16 hours a day, none spoken for", and either a label for the bar or no bar.
- **A stray label under the map.** "Spoke B power · schematic layout, not geographic positions" sits under the map on every screenshot, including day 11 when nothing about Spoke B power is selected. It reads as a status line about power.
- **Unticked prep boxes are nearly invisible** (dark box on dark background).
- **The tutorial is not on the front door.** M2R-10 shipped with the tutorial disabled pending data; the tutorial scenario is on main (M2R-08). Turning it on belongs with M2R-17 (mentor messages).
- **Refreshed screenshots for the gate.** The committed set predates the J4 fuel line (the contracts card still says the host nation has hub fuel) and plain names. The gate needs the opening, day 1, day 11 of approach 1 and the debrief from main after M2R-12 and M2R-17.

### Seen and left alone
- The title's small "KTHQ" is the imprint (SPEC: a KTHQ product); fine.
- Site names "Hub" and "Spoke B" read as names ("water for Hub"). Nick kept unit and site names for now (design doc 12); not changing scenario names before the gate.
- Approach 1 leads days 16 to 30 with the same out-of-scope billing because the log never decides it. In play the player decides it and the lead moves on; correct behaviour.
- Quiet days 4 to 10 in approach 1 mean Continue goes straight from day 3 to the morning of day 11, when the first consequence shows. That meets the M2R test (a visible consequence by day 10).

## Addendum, 9 Nov 2026 (second scheduled run): the J4 is not on screen

Writing the gate script (M2R-21, docs/PLAYTEST.md) turned up a gap the first walk missed. On today's Sim, approach 1's day 10 gap is "nobody owned the fuel": the dependency checklist names the J4 (ACSA) as fuel's usual owner, and asking the J4 on day 1 is the one-decision fix (golden `approach1-with-j4`). The game shows neither. `RequirementDependency.UsualOwner` and `OwnerEngaged` are not bound in the requirement panel, and no screen sends `EngageJ4Action`; M2R-17's acceptance lists finance, the staff meeting, the SCO and vendor visits but not the J4. A player at the gate would be told in the debrief that they never asked the J4, with no way to have asked. That fails R8 (it teaches itself) at the exact point the lesson is meant to land. New Codex row M2R-22 (checklist owner and Ask the J4), unblocked; the gate (M2R-23) waits on it.
