## End answers

Who was I? The contracting officer for an operating main-island hub supporting three hundred people, with Avery and Novak in the shop. I understood contracting generally, but I was new to this game's rules and controls.

What was I trying to do? Keep the hub supplied for sixteen days: water and forklifts out no more than one day each, no missed waste-removal days, and at least 95% mission output.

Did I manage it? Yes. All four lines held. I bought water from Island Mart for days 2–8, used the staff meeting for warning, asked Dunn about forklift operators, ordered Coastal Rentals with operators and maintenance, and got Main Island Sanitation started on day 13. I issued a cure notice for the dining vendor. I knowingly accepted the quoted one-day forklift gap, which used the commander's allowance and cost mission output.

What one change would have helped most? Make the COR explanation match the selected COR. On the waste purchase I selected Lane, while the paragraph still talked about Pratt. On the forklift purchase the paragraph talked about Pratt even when the selector said None. That made me question whether I had actually assigned the person I meant to assign. Correct current-status wording would help more than adding another tutorial paragraph.
