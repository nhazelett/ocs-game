52. Day 20, debrief (0437-0460). Easily the best screen in the game. Maj Cole names the worst gap, then lists every gap tagged Avoidable / Discoverable / Unforeseeable, with "Trace the evidence" showing the causal chain back to day 0. Scores: mission output 94.1%, throughput 77.5%, endurance 99.1%, resilience 100%. Obligated $654,301 / incurred $337,298 / unobligated incurred $2,418.75. Unauthorized commitments 0, overdue UCAs 1, HHQ inquiries 1, surveys 0, shop hours worked 111. 4 gaps: 3 avoidable, 1 unforeseeable. It also finally explained the day-19 mystery: "Higher HQ asked why Harbor Bottling water at Hub is past due for definitization."
53. The debrief also revealed a day-4 line I never saw during play: "Harbor Equipment Co is short of forklifts this week". That is presumably why they declined and why they went grey later. It was logged but never surfaced to me where I was looking.
54. Thing I wanted to click and did not: "Menu" (top right) - several times, because I wanted a rules page, a glossary or a save. There is no help anywhere in the game. Also "Developer tools" (top right, on every screen including the very first) - it is in the most prominent corner of the title area and is plainly not for players.
