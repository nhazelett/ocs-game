## Artifact it rests on

`backlog/QUESTIONS.md` as of 8 Nov 2026: five entries, all questions, zero objections. Expected volume is a handful per milestone. With one or two objections in a period, "all accepted" or "all rejected" is the likely result by arithmetic, not a sign of anything.

## Proposed alternative

Always report the counts. Call the mechanism decorative only when four or more objections were filed in the period, or when the same 100% pattern appears in two reviews in a row.

## What happens if ignored

False "decorative" flags in the first milestones, which teach everyone to skim past the check by the time it would mean something.
