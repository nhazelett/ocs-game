# OCS: Lines of Support

A training strategy game about Operational Contract Support and contingency contracting. A KTHQ product. Work in progress: it is not finished and not yet released.

This is an unofficial, personal project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the Department of Defense or any military service. The islands, units, vendors and people in it are fictional.
